title: What's new in {{product}} {{version}}
version: 0.5.0
---

- **Automix**: music now flows like a DJ set. Songs blend into each other on the beat, with smooth transition effects, and albums meant to play without gaps stay gapless. Turn it on or off, and pick Smooth, Energetic or Minimal transitions, from the Automix button on the player.
- **Mix anything**: press **Mix** on an album, artist, playlist or music library to hear it as one continuous mix in a smart order. **Endless** keeps it going with similar songs from your library.
- **Your DJ**: one tap on Home starts a personal set from your own library, in themed blocks like On Repeat, Throwbacks and Forgotten favourites. Each song says why it was picked. Press **Switch it up** to change direction, or type a request.
- **Radio Host**: like listening to the radio, without the ads. Turn it on in **Settings → Playback** and a host introduces songs, shares a music fact, the time or the weather now and then. The music dips while it talks and never under the vocals, with captions on screen.
- **Play rules**: limit what the DJ plays by genre, artist, decade, tempo and more, and save rule sets like "Focus" or "Workout". Admins can set rules a profile can't change.
- **AI your way**: admins can connect a local AI, free services or paid ones in order of preference, for smarter requests and host talk. If one stops working, Vibe quietly moves to the next. With nothing connected, everything still works using built-in lines and your device's voice.
- **Better beat and key detection**: tempo and key are more accurate, and your library is re-checked gradually in the background.
- **Advanced DJ**: the full two-deck DJ booth is still here; switch it on under **Settings → Playback**.
