version: 0.4.0

- **Say hello to Vibe**: Kinora is now Vibe, with a new look from top to bottom and a new logo: a glowing disc with a V through it. Your libraries, settings and apps carry on as before.
- **Your library first**: Home opens with Continue Watching, then what's next and what's new. Press **Customize** to hide or reorder rows for your profile, and find every library pinned in the sidebar in the order you like.
- **Less clicking**: every title has one main button (Play, Resume, or Play the next episode), and audio and subtitles are one click away in the player, with a live preview of how captions will look.
- **Browse your way**: posters, a list or A–Z, with filters and a jump bar. Each library remembers how you left it.
- **A real TV guide**: channel numbers, a line showing what's on now, type a number to jump to a channel, and watch, record or set a reminder from the side panel.
- **Music that keeps its place**: lyrics and the queue side by side, with speed, sleep timer and chapters for audiobooks and podcasts.
- **Pick your skin**: Rose, Aurora or Ember, each in dark and light, under **Settings → Display**. The logo follows your skin.
- **Calmer by default**: trailers no longer play on their own unless you turn them on, and everything works with a keyboard, clear focus outlines and reduced motion.

See [What is {{product}}?](help:welcome) for a tour.
