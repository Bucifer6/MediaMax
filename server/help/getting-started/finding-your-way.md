## The sidebar

On a computer the sidebar is on the left. On a phone, tap **Library** at the bottom (or the menu button at the top) to open it.

| Item | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Rows picked for you: Continue Watching, Next Up, Top 10, recommendations and recently added. |
| **Search** | Search every library at once. |
| **Discover** / **My requests** | Find titles that aren't on the server yet and ask for them (only when requests are on). |
| **Libraries** | Each library the admin shared with you, such as Movies, TV Shows or Music. |
| **Live TV**, **TV Guide**, **Recordings** | Live channels, the programme guide and your recordings. |
| **My List**, **Calendar** | Things you saved for later, and upcoming episodes of your shows. |
| **DJ**, **Podcasts**, **Playlists**, **Collections** | Music mixes, podcasts and lists of titles. |
| **Help** | This Help Centre. |

## Searching

Press <kbd>/</kbd> anywhere (except while typing in a box) to jump to **Search**, or use the search box at the top. Results come from every library you can see.

## The account menu

Click your picture in the top-right corner for:

- **Switch profile**, when your account has profiles.
- **Your year**: your viewing and listening stats.
- **Settings**: playback, subtitles, audio, theme and devices.
- **Link a device**: sign in a TV or phone with a code.
- **Admin dashboard** (admins only) and **Sign out**.

## Item pages

Open any title to see its details. From there you can play or resume it, add it to **My List**, rate it, download it (when allowed), pick an edition or extra, and report a problem.
