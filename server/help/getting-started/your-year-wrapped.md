Open your account menu and choose **Your year**, then **Play your story**.

## The story {#story}

Cards play one after another, like stories on social apps:

- how much time you spent watching and listening;
- your top titles, genres and the people (actors and directors) you watched most;
- your binge record — the most episodes of one show in a single day;
- your **film personality**, a light-hearted label based on what and when you watch;
- a day-by-day heatmap of the year and your biggest day;
- your top music.

## Moving around {#controls}

| | Phone | Computer | TV remote |
|---|---|---|---|
| Next card | Tap the right side or swipe left | → | Right |
| Previous card | Tap the left third or swipe right | ← | Left |
| Pause | Touch and hold | Space | OK |
| Close | × | Esc | Back |

Cards move on by themselves every few seconds. With **Reduce motion** turned on in your device's settings, the story doesn't animate or auto-advance.

## Save and share {#share}

- **Save card** saves the card you're looking at as a tall image (1080 × 1920, the size stories use).
- **Share all** opens your phone's share sheet with every card, ready for a story or a chat. Where sharing files isn't supported, the images are downloaded instead.

The images are made on your device. They only contain your own stats and the posters of what you watched — nothing about anyone else on the server.

You can also pick another year or **All time** at the top of the page.
