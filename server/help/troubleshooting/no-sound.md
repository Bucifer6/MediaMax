## No sound at all

1. Check the player isn't muted (press <kbd>M</kbd>) and the volume is up (<kbd>↑</kbd>).
2. Check the device's own volume, and that the browser tab isn't muted.
3. Try another audio track from the **Audio** menu (or press <kbd>A</kbd>). Some files have a broken or commentary-only first track.
4. With a soundbar or AV receiver: switch it to the right input, and check the TV's sound output setting (for example *HDMI ARC* or *PCM*).
5. Turn off **Night mode**, **Dialogue boost** and **Normalise volume** in **Settings → Playback → Audio** and try again.

If one title has no sound on every device, the file is likely the problem: [tell your admin](help:problem-with-a-title).

## Wrong language

Choose the right track in the **Audio** menu. To get it automatically next time, set **Settings → Playback → Preferred audio language**. Each profile has its own setting.

## Dialogue too quiet, effects too loud

Turn on **Dialogue boost** and **Night mode** in **Settings → Playback → Audio** (or in the player's audio menu).

## Sound is out of sync with the picture

- **Always late on this device** (often a soundbar or receiver): set **Settings → Playback → Audio → Lip-sync on this device**.
- **Only in one video**: use **Sync & delay** in the player's audio or subtitle menu.

## No Dolby Atmos or surround

Browsers often report stereo only, even with a receiver connected, so the server sends stereo. Use the TV or phone app for surround. Atmos also becomes plain surround when the server converts the audio or when listening comforts are on. **Settings → Playback → Audio** shows what your device reported; *Stats for nerds* (<kbd>I</kbd>) shows what you're getting and why.
