You can download a title when your server admin allows downloads for your account. If there is no download button, downloads are off for you.

## On a phone or tablet (iOS, Android app)

1. Open the movie, episode, album or song.
2. Tap the download button.
3. Find your downloads under the **Downloads** tab. They play without a connection, and your progress is sent to the server the next time you're online.

Choose the size in **Settings → Download quality**:

| Quality | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Original** | The untouched file: best quality, largest size. |
| **1080p** | Tablets and TVs. |
| **720p** | Phones. |
| **480p** | Smallest size. |

The converted sizes (1080p, 720p, 480p) are made by the server while downloading, so their progress shows only the amount downloaded so far. On iPhone and iPad, files the system player can't open (such as MKV) are downloaded as 1080p automatically.

**Settings → Downloads** shows how much space downloads use. Delete them from the **Downloads** tab.

## In a browser

On a title's page, the download button saves the file to your computer. For videos you can pick **Original file** or a smaller **1080p**, **720p** or **480p (MP4)** copy. Play it with any media player.

## Tips

- Download on Wi-Fi: originals can be tens of gigabytes.
- Live TV channels and titles streamed from an IPTV provider can't be downloaded.
