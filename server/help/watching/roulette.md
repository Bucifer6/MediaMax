Can't decide what to watch? **Roulette** spins a reel of posters and stops on something from your library.

## Where to find it

- **Home**: the **Can't decide? Spin** button above the rows.
- **Movie and TV libraries**: the **Roulette** button at the top (it keeps the genre you're browsing).
- **Search**: before you type anything.
- **TV remote**: press the **Green** button anywhere outside the player.
- **Phone and TV apps**: **Can't decide? Spin** on Home.

## Movie or series

- **Movie** picks a film.
- **Series** picks a show and the right episode: the **next one** if you've started it, otherwise **season 1, episode 1**. Turn on **Random episode** for shows you can dip into anywhere, like sitcoms.

## Moods

Tap a mood for a quick start: **Feel-good**, **Edge of your seat**, **Mind-bending**, **Laugh**, **Family night** (family-friendly ratings only) and **Short & sweet** (under about 1 h 35 m for films, half-hour episodes for series).

## Filters

Open **Filters** to narrow it down by library, genres, runtime, decade and rating, or to pick only **Unwatched** titles or titles **From My List**.

**Favour highly rated & new** (on by default) makes better-rated titles and recently added ones more likely. Roulette never suggests anything you gave a thumbs down or marked **Not interested**, and it only ever picks titles your profile is allowed to see.

## After the spin

- **Play** starts the pick (or the chosen episode).
- **Trailer** plays the trailer, when there is one.
- **Spin again** for another go.
- **Not this one** skips the pick for the rest of this visit and spins again.
- **Marathon** plays the pick, then keeps going with random titles — see [Random marathon](help:random-marathon).

If you have **reduced motion** turned on in your device settings, the reel is skipped and the pick simply fades in.
