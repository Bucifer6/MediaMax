Recording needs live TV channels on the server and an account that may record. If you can't see **Recordings** in the sidebar, ask your admin.

## Recording a programme

1. Open the **TV Guide** (or the Live TV page) and choose a programme.
2. Choose **Record** for this showing only, or **Record series** to record every new episode.

Recordings start a minute early and end a few minutes late by default, in case the broadcast runs over.

## The Recordings page

**Recordings** lists:

- **Scheduled** recordings, which you can cancel.
- **Completed** recordings, which play like any other video, with resume.
- **Series rules**: edit which days and channels a rule records, turn it on or off, or delete it. If two rules want the same tuner at once, **Make top priority** decides which wins.

## Skipping ad breaks

If your admin has turned on commercial detection, finished recordings get **Skip ad break** buttons. On a TV remote, the **Yellow** button skips too.

## Where are recordings kept?

On the server, in the recordings folder your admin chose. They count towards the server's disk space, so delete ones you've watched.
