The **Play to** button (the cast icon in the player, the music player and on title pages) lists the devices you can send to:

| Device | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Google Cast** (Chromecast, Google TV, Nest speakers) | Chrome and Edge on a computer, and the Android and iOS apps. |
| **AirPlay** (Apple TV, AirPlay speakers and TVs) | Safari on a Mac, iPhone or iPad, and the iOS app. |
| **TVs, AV receivers and other DLNA players** | Found on your home network by the server. |
| **Sonos** | Music only. Albums and playlists go to the Sonos queue. |
| **Kodi** | Kodi must allow remote control over HTTP. |

## Sending something

1. Start playing, or open the title.
2. Press **Play to** and pick a device.
3. Your phone or browser becomes the remote: pause, seek, change the volume or stop. Progress is saved as usual.

To stop, press **Play to** again and disconnect, or press stop in the bar at the bottom of the screen.

## Can't see your device?

- The device and the server must be on the same home network. Devices on a guest Wi-Fi or another VLAN usually aren't found.
- Google Cast only appears in browsers that support it (Chrome, Edge) and in the apps.
- AirPlay only appears in Safari and the iOS app.
- TVs, Sonos and Kodi are found by the server, not by your phone. If they never appear, ask your admin to check **Admin → Play to devices**.

## Quality

The device gets the original file when it can play it. Otherwise the server converts it into a format almost every device understands.
