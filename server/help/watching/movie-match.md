**Movie Match** helps a group agree on something to watch. Everyone gets the **same deck of cards in the same order** on their own phone. Swipe right for yes, left for no, and up for a **super like**. As soon as everyone says yes to the same title, every screen shows **It's a match!**

## Starting a session

1. Open **Movie Match** — from the **Movie Match** button on Home (or **Can't decide?** in Search), on the TV, or in the phone app.
2. Choose what to match: **Movies**, **Shows** or **Both**, and optionally libraries, genres and a maximum runtime.
3. Choose when it counts as a match:
   - **Everyone says yes** (the default).
   - **Most people say yes**: more than half the group, and a super like counts twice.
4. Leave **Only things nobody in the group has seen** on to skip anything anyone has watched (or started, for shows).
5. Choose **Start matching**. A six-letter code, a QR code and a link appear.

Starting on the TV works well: the TV shows the code and the QR code in large type, who has joined, and who is still swiping.

## Joining

- **Scan the QR code** with your phone's camera, or open the link. You sign in as yourself (or your profile) if you haven't already.
- **Type the code** under **Movie Match → Join a session**, in a browser or the phone app.

Everyone needs their own account or profile on this server.

## Swiping

| Action | Touch | Keyboard / TV remote |
|---|---|---|
| Yes | Swipe right, or the heart button | → (Right) |
| No | Swipe left, or the ✕ button | ← (Left) |
| Super like | Swipe up, or the star button | ↑ (Up) |

On a TV, the deck takes the arrow keys while it is selected; press **Down** to reach the buttons and the rest of the page.

The deck is a mix of highly rated titles, things added recently, titles like the ones the group has been watching, and a few surprises. When someone gets near the end, more cards are added for everyone. If you lose your connection or close the app, open the session again — you carry on where you left off.

## It's a match!

Every device in the group celebrates at the same moment. You can:

- **Play on (the TV)** — start the match on the device that started the session. This only works for matches, and only while that device has the session open.
- **Play here** — start it on the device in your hand.
- **Keep swiping** — find a few more, then pick from the list.

The **Matches** tab lists every match, the most loved first (super likes count double, a no counts against).

## Age ratings and kids profiles

The deck only ever contains titles **every member** is allowed to see. The strictest profile in the group sets the limit: if a kids profile joins, anything above its age rating disappears from the deck for everyone, and libraries someone can't open are left out. Titles anyone marked **Not interested** or gave a thumbs down never appear.

## Ending a session

The person who started it can choose **End session**; everyone else can **Leave**. Sessions also end on their own two hours after the last activity.
