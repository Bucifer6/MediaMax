## Ask

On the search page, switch from **Search** to **Ask** and describe what you want, for example:

- *Something funny under 90 minutes we haven't seen*
- *Romanian comedies from the 2000s* or *comedii românești din anii 2000*
- *Like Inception but lighter*
- *Filme de groază din anii 80*, *seriale polițiste bine cotate*

Above the results, **Understood as** shows how your question was read, for example *Comedy · ≤ 90 min · Unwatched*.
Tap or click a chip to remove it and widen the search. "Haven't seen" means titles **you** haven't watched; you only
ever see titles from libraries you have access to.

Ask understands running time, decades and years, genres and moods, ratings, the spoken language, actors and directors
("with Tom Hanks", "regizat de Cristian Mungiu") and "like ⟨a title⟩". If your server has an AI model connected, it
also understands looser descriptions.

## Previously on…

If you come back to a series after a while, or start a new season, the episode page (and the start of the episode)
shows a **Previously on…** card with a short recap of the last episodes you watched. It never mentions anything after
the last episode you watched. Dismiss it with **×**. If the episode has its own recap segment, **Play the recap clip**
plays just that part. No card? **Recap the story so far** on the episode page makes one anyway.

## Auto-generated subtitles

Some titles may have a subtitle called **Auto-generated (AI)**. Your server made it from the dialogue with
speech-to-text (and translated it when the film speaks another language) because no subtitle could be found online.
It is usually good but can get names or quiet lines wrong; a downloaded subtitle is preferred when one exists.
