**Multiview** shows up to four live channels in a grid. Only one plays sound at a time.

## Opening multiview

Start a live channel, then press the **Multiview** button in the player. The channel you were watching becomes the first tile.

## Using it

- **Add channel** opens a search: type a channel or programme name and pick one.
- Click or select a tile to give it the sound. On a keyboard, press <kbd>1</kbd>–<kbd>4</kbd>, or <kbd>Tab</kbd> to move to the next tile.
- Each tile has buttons to **Change channel**, **Remove** it, or **Open full screen** in the normal player.
- <kbd>F</kbd> toggles fullscreen, <kbd>Esc</kbd> leaves multiview.

## Good to know

Each tile is a separate stream from the server, so four tiles use four times the bandwidth, and four tuner or provider connections. If a tile shows **Retry**, the server or your IPTV provider may have run out of connections.
