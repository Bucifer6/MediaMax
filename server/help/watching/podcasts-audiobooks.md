## Podcasts

Open **Podcasts** in the sidebar.

1. Choose **Add a podcast** and search by name, or paste the podcast's feed (RSS) address.
2. Subscribe. New episodes appear on the podcast's page, and your progress is saved like any other title.
3. Turn on **Auto-download** if you want new episodes stored on the server as soon as they come out.

Each podcast's menu has **Refresh feed**, **Copy feed URL** and **Unsubscribe**.

## Audiobooks

Audiobooks live in an audiobook library, grouped by author and series. Press **Start listening** or **Resume**; books with several files play as one, in order, and the page shows how much of each part you've listened to.

In the music player:

- **Chapters** jumps to a chapter (when the book has them).
- **Bookmarks** saves the current position with a note, to come back to later.
- The **sleep timer** stops playback after 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes, or at the end of the chapter.

## Where's my audiobook?

Audiobooks need an audiobook library on the server. If you can't see one, ask your admin to add it or give you access.
