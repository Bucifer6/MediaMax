Photo and home video libraries appear in the sidebar under **Libraries**, like any other library, when your server admin has shared them with you.

## Photos

- Open a photo library to see its folders and photos as square tiles.
- Open a photo to see it larger, and press **View full size** to see the whole picture.
- On a TV, move between photos with the D-pad and press **OK** to open one.

## Home videos

Home video libraries show your own clips (holiday videos, phone recordings) in wide tiles. They play like any other video, with resume and *Continue Watching*.

## Can't see photos?

Your admin may have turned photos off for the whole server, or not given your account access to the photo library.
