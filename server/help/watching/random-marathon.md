## Random marathon

A **Random marathon** plays a random title, and when it ends plays another random one with the same filters — on and on. A short **10-second countdown** card shows what's next; choose **Cancel** to stop after the current title.

Start one with:

- **Shuffle play** on a movie or TV library page (it keeps the genre you're browsing), or on the Roulette page.
- **Marathon** on a Roulette pick.

For TV libraries a marathon plays **random episodes, forever** — ideal for sitcoms. Within a marathon nothing is played twice, and titles you gave a thumbs down or marked **Not interested** are skipped.

## Sleep timer

Press the **clock** button at the top of the player (the **moon** button in the phone and TV apps) and choose:

- **30**, **60** or **90 minutes**
- **End of this film** (or episode)
- **After 2, 3 or 4 titles** (during a marathon)

The sound fades out over the last 30 seconds, then playback stops. Your place is saved.

## Are you still watching?

So the server isn't streaming to a sleeping viewer all night, a marathon asks **Are you still watching?** after **2 titles in a row** or **4 hours** without anyone touching the remote, screen or keyboard. Choose **Keep watching** to carry on. If nobody answers within a minute, playback pauses and the player closes; the title you were in keeps its position and is **not** marked as watched.

To turn the question off (or back on), open the sleep timer menu and choose **Ask "Are you still watching?"**. In a browser the choice is remembered on that device.
