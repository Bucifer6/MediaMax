## Playing music

Open a music library, pick an album, artist or playlist and press **Play** (or **Shuffle**). The music player stays at the bottom of the screen while you browse. Open it for:

- **Queue**: what plays next. Remove or reorder songs.
- **Lyrics**: synced lyrics scroll with the song when they are available.
- **More options**: **Start radio**, **Artist radio**, **Album radio** and **Go to artist**.

## Radio

A radio station keeps playing songs similar to the one you started from, from your own library.

## Crossfade and gapless

In **Settings → Playback → Music**:

- **Crossfade** blends the end of one song into the next (0–12 seconds). At *off*, songs play gapless. Albums played in order always stay gapless.
- **Volume levelling (ReplayGain)** evens out loudness between songs. *Automatic* uses album levels when you play an album in order and song levels otherwise.

## Other music apps

You can use Subsonic music apps such as Symfonium, Feishin, play:Sub, Amperfy or Substreamer. The connection details are in **Settings → Security**. Sign in with your username and password, or an API key if you use two-factor sign-in.

## In the car

The phone apps support CarPlay and Android Auto for music, audiobooks and podcasts.
