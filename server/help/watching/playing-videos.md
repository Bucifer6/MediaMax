## Playing and resuming

Press **Play** on a title. If you stopped part-way through, the button says **Resume** and shows where you were. Progress is saved on the server, so you can stop on the TV and carry on on your phone. Half-watched titles appear in **Continue Watching** on Home; the next episode of a show you're watching appears in **Next Up**.

## Player controls

Move the mouse or tap the screen to show the controls. On a keyboard:

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Space</kbd> or <kbd>K</kbd> | Play / pause |
| <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> (or <kbd>J</kbd> <kbd>L</kbd>) | Back / forward 10 seconds (hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> for 30) |
| <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> | Volume |
| <kbd>0</kbd>–<kbd>9</kbd> | Jump to 0–90 % of the video |
| <kbd>F</kbd> / <kbd>M</kbd> / <kbd>P</kbd> | Fullscreen / mute / picture-in-picture |
| <kbd>C</kbd> / <kbd>A</kbd> | Next subtitle / next audio track |
| <kbd>N</kbd> | Next episode |
| <kbd>&lt;</kbd> <kbd>&gt;</kbd> | Slower / faster (0.5× to 2×) |
| <kbd>I</kbd> | *Stats for nerds* |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Close a menu, or leave the player |

Press <kbd>?</kbd> in the player to see this list on screen. On phones, double-tap the left or right side of the picture to skip 10 seconds.

## Skipping intros, recaps and credits

When the server knows where an intro, recap, credits or ad break is, a **Skip** button appears. To skip intros automatically, turn on **Settings → Playback → Automatically skip intros**.

## Up Next

Near the end of an episode an **Up Next** card counts down to the next one. Turn this off with **Settings → Playback → Autoplay next episode**.

## Chapters, editions and extras

- **Chapters**: the chapter button in the player lists the file's chapters.
- **Editions**: if a movie has several versions (for example a Director's Cut), choose one on its page before playing.
- **Extras**: trailers, featurettes and deleted scenes appear on the title's page.

## Cinema mode

If your admin has turned on cinema mode, a movie started from the beginning may play a couple of trailers and a short intro first, like at the cinema. You can skip straight to the movie.

## Something wrong?

Press <kbd>I</kbd> for *Stats for nerds*: it shows how the video reaches you and, if the server is converting it, why. See [Why is it transcoding?](help:why-transcoding).
