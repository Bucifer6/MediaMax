**DJ** (in the sidebar) mixes songs from your music library like a DJ would: it matches tempo and key, and blends one song into the next on the beat.

## Asking the AI DJ

Under **What do you feel like?**, describe the mix, for example *"chilled 90s house for a Sunday morning"* or *"upbeat rock, 1 hour"*, and press **Mix now**. The DJ picks songs that fit the mood, decade, genre, tempo and length you asked for.

If your server has the DJ voice turned on, a host announces songs between tracks. Turn the voice off with the **Voice** button.

## Setting it up yourself

Choose **Or set it up yourself** to pick:

- **Start from**: a song, album, artist or playlist, or leave it empty to start from your favourites.
- **Genres**: none selected means your whole music library.
- **Energy**: the shape of the mix, for example building up or staying calm.
- **Length** and tempo (**BPM**).

## While a mix plays

- **Love** a song to add it to your favourites and steer future mixes.
- **Skip next** skips the upcoming song; the DJ learns from skips.
- The **crossfader** and **mix point** show the transition; you can start the next transition now.
- **Save as playlist** keeps the mix; **End mix** stops it.

A small dot on **DJ** in the sidebar shows a mix is playing while you browse.

## Why are some songs never chosen?

The DJ needs to analyse each song once (tempo, key, energy). New songs are analysed in the background; press **Analyse now** on the DJ page if many are waiting.
