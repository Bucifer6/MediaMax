**Live TV** in the sidebar lists the channels you can watch, with what's on now and next. **TV Guide** shows the programme grid.

## Watching a channel

Pick a channel to start it. In the live player:

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> (or <kbd>Page Up</kbd> / <kbd>Page Down</kbd>, **Channel +/−**) | Previous / next channel |
| <kbd>0</kbd>–<kbd>9</kbd> | Type a channel number |
| <kbd>L</kbd> or <kbd>I</kbd> | Channel list |
| <kbd>G</kbd> | TV guide |
| <kbd>C</kbd> | Captions on / off (when the channel has them) |
| <kbd>Space</kbd> / <kbd>F</kbd> / <kbd>M</kbd> | Pause / fullscreen / mute |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Leave |

## The TV guide

Scroll through channels and times, or use **Day**, **Earlier** and **Later**. Filter by category. Choose a programme to see its details, then:

- **Watch live** if it's on now.
- **Watch from start**, if the channel offers catch-up.
- **Record** or **Record series**, if recordings are on for your account (see [Recording TV shows](help:recordings)).

## Catch-up

Some channels keep recent programmes for a few days. Those show **Catch-up** on the Live TV page and in the guide. While watching catch-up, <kbd>←</kbd> and <kbd>→</kbd> skip 10 seconds.

## Favourites

Mark channels as favourites to keep them at the top. Favourite channels can also appear as a row on Home.

## Can't see Live TV?

Live TV only appears when the server has channels and your account may watch them. Kids profiles don't get Live TV unless the admin allows it. If a channel won't play, see [Live TV won't play](help:live-tv-problems).
