## My List

Press **+** (My List) on any title to save it for later. Everything you saved is under **My List** in the sidebar and in the *My List* row on Home. Press it again to remove the title.

Each profile has its own list.

## Rating and recommendations

Rate titles with thumbs up or down on their page. Ratings shape the *Recommended for you* and *Because you watched…* rows on Home. Choose **Not interested** to stop a title being suggested.

## Watched and unwatched

Titles you finish are marked as watched automatically. You can also mark a title, season or episode as watched or unwatched from its page, for example to tidy up *Continue Watching*.

## Calendar

**Calendar** in the sidebar lists new and upcoming episodes of the shows in your libraries, with a *This week* view. When a new episode of a show you follow arrives, the bell at the top of the screen tells you.

## Your year

**Your year** (in the account menu) shows what you watched and listened to most, Wrapped-style.
