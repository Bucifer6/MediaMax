On a TV, {{product}} switches to **TV mode**: bigger text, large buttons and a clear highlight around whatever is selected, so you can use it from the sofa with a remote.

## Moving around

- **Arrow keys (D-pad)** move the highlight to the nearest item in that direction.
- **OK** (Enter) opens or presses the highlighted item.
- **Back** closes a menu or dialog, leaves the player, or goes back a page. On the home screen, Back shows **Press Back again to exit**: press it again within 2 seconds to close the app.

Rows of posters scroll by themselves as you move along them. When a dialog is open, the highlight stays inside it until you close it.

## Typing

Select a search box or text field and press **OK** to bring up the TV's keyboard. To sign in without typing a password, use a code from your phone: see [Installing the apps and signing in on a TV](help:apps-and-devices).

## Turning TV mode on or off

TV mode turns on by itself in the Samsung, LG and Android TV apps and in most TV browsers. On any other device, open the server address with `?tv=1` at the end (for example `http://192.168.1.10:8484/?tv=1`). It is remembered. Use `?tv=0` to turn it off again.

## Remote control buttons

Play, pause, colour buttons, channel buttons and numbers all work. See [Remote control buttons](help:remote-buttons). **Settings → Playback → This device** lists them on screen in TV mode.
