Any phone can drive {{product}} on a TV — no extra app required.

## Pair your phone {#pair}

1. On the TV, open your account menu and choose **Use your phone as remote**. While a video plays you can also press **Info** (or **Blue**) and pick **Use your phone as remote** in the panel.
2. The TV shows a QR code and a 6-character code. Point your phone's camera at the QR code and open the link — or open **/pair** on your phone in a browser and type the code.
3. Sign in if asked, then tap **Pair in this browser**. If you have the {{product}} app installed, **Open in the app instead** pairs the app.

Codes are valid for a few minutes and work once; the TV shows a fresh one after each phone pairs.

### Someone else's phone {#guests}

A phone signed in with **another account** can pair too, but only after the person signed in on the TV taps **Allow** on screen. A guest phone can:

- control playback (play, pause, seek, volume, audio and subtitle tracks, skip intro);
- browse and start titles that the TV's account is allowed to watch.

It can't see the TV account's history, ratings or settings, and guest pairings end on their own after a few hours.

## The remote {#remote}

- **Touch-pad** — swipe up, down, left or right to move the highlight; keep your finger moving to step repeatedly. Tap to select (OK).
- **Back**, **Home** and **Info** buttons.
- **Play / pause**, **−10 s** and **+10 s**, and **volume** (the player's own volume).
- **Type on the TV** — whatever you type goes into the field that's selected on the TV (search, sign-in). With nothing selected it opens Search.

The touch-pad and keyboard only work with a TV you've paired by QR code. Your own other devices still appear in the list for playback control.

## Second screen {#second-screen}

While the TV plays, the **Now playing** tab shows:

- a scrubber with preview thumbnails — drag to seek;
- **Skip intro / credits** when the TV shows the Skip button;
- audio and subtitle choices, applied on the TV;
- chapters, the cast and crew, the season's episodes and what's up next;
- **What's this song?** — see [What's this song?](help:whats-this-song).

## Play on TV {#browse}

The **Browse** tab searches the TV's library. Tap a movie or episode to start it on the TV; tap a show to pick an episode.

## Unpair {#unpair}

- On the phone: the **×** at the top of the remote.
- On the TV: **Use your phone as remote** lists paired phones with an **Unpair** button.

Signing out on either device also ends the pairing.
