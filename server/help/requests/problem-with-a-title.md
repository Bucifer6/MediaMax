If a title has a problem in the file itself (wrong language, missing subtitles, a corrupt episode, the wrong movie), tell your admin so they can fix or replace it.

## Sending an issue

1. Open the title's page (for a show, the show, season or episode).
2. Choose **Report issue**.
3. Pick the **Problem type**: *Video*, *Audio*, *Subtitles* or *Other*. For shows, pick the **Season** and **Episode**.
4. **Describe the problem**, for example *"Episode 4 has no sound after 20 minutes"*, and send it.

## Following up

Your issues are under **My issues** in the sidebar. You'll see replies there, and when the issue is **Resolved**. If the problem comes back, you can **Reopen** it.

## Before you send one

Many playback problems are about your device or connection, not the file. Try these first:

- [Buffering and slow streams](help:buffering)
- [No sound or the wrong audio](help:no-sound)
- [Subtitles missing or out of sync](help:subtitle-problems)
