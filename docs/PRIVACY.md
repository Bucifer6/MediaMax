# Kinora apps — Privacy policy

_Last updated: [date of publication]_

Kinora apps (for iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Android, Android TV, Fire TV, Samsung, LG, Hisense VIDAA and Roku) are clients for a Kinora media server that **you or someone you know runs**. This policy explains what the apps do with your information.

## The short version

- The apps do **not** collect, sell or share any personal information with the app's developer or with third parties.
- There are **no ads, analytics or tracking** in the apps.
- Everything you do in the app is sent **only to the Kinora server you connect to**, which is run by its owner, not by us.

## What the apps store on your device

- The address of the server you connect to.
- A sign-in token for that server (not your password).
- A random device identifier the app creates, so the server can tell your devices apart.
- Your app settings (e.g. subtitle style, playback quality), recent searches, and a cached copy of the server's
  look (name, logo, colours) and of artwork, so the app starts quickly.
- Downloaded media for offline viewing, if you use downloads.

You can remove all of this by signing out and uninstalling the app.

## What is sent to your Kinora server

To work, the app sends the server you choose: your sign-in details, your device's name (as set in its settings), model and type, the app version, what you play and your playback position, your ratings, favourites and lists, a profile picture if you choose one from your photos, and the network address your device connects from. The server's owner can see this information (for example on the server's dashboard), as with any self-hosted media server. Their own privacy practices apply to it; contact them with questions about it.

## Optional services

If the server owner enables features that use outside services (for example Trakt, Last.fm, OpenSubtitles or
metadata providers), those requests are made **by the server**, not by the app, and only with the server owner's or
your consent where applicable.

A few things open another site **only when you tap them**: signing in with an identity provider your server uses
(the provider's sign-in page opens in a browser), linking Trakt or Last.fm (their websites), a title's trailer
(often on YouTube), and the server's help or support pages. That site's own privacy policy applies.

## Other services built into the apps

- **Chromecast (iPhone, iPad and Android phones):** the phone apps include Google's Cast SDK so you can play to a
  Chromecast or Google TV. It looks for Cast devices on your local network and, on Android, works through Google
  Play services. Google's privacy policy applies to the SDK.
- **Fonts on Samsung, LG and Hisense TVs:** these apps show your server's web app. If the server's theme uses a web
  font (some built-in themes do; the default theme doesn't), the TV downloads that font from Google Fonts, which
  sees the TV's network address.
- **The Roku channel** looks for Kinora servers on your local network; that traffic stays on your network.

## Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13. Server owners can create restricted profiles with parental ratings.
